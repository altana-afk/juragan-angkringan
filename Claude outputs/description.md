# Juragan Angkringan Simulator

**Short description (itch.io tagline):**
Run a Javanese street-food cart from one rickety stall to the neighborhood's favorite night spot.

---

An angkringan is a humble food cart found on Indonesian street corners: rice parcels wrapped in banana leaf, satay grilled over charcoal, sweet tea in glass mugs, and a bench where neighbors sit and talk until late. In Juragan Angkringan Simulator, you inherit one of these carts at the end of a quiet alley, and it's up to you to bring it back to life.

Every evening starts with prep. Buy ingredients at prices that shift day to day, cook the menu, and set your own prices. Then open up and watch the customers come: students counting coins, workers heading home, ride-hail drivers on a break, families out for dinner. Seats fill, queues form, and if the fried rice runs out, people notice.

## What you do

- Buy ingredients, cook portions, and set prices. Cheaper sells faster but earns less.
- Serve 7 kinds of customers, each with their own budget, patience, and taste.
- Watch the weather. Rain brings demand for hot ginger drinks; scorching nights sell iced tea.
- Unlock 30 menu items, from nasi kucing and chicken satay to charcoal coffee, plus one secret recipe.
- Upgrade the cart, the kitchen, and the seating with 35 upgrades you can see on the map.
- Move between 5 locations: Village Street, Near Campus, Market Edge, Station Front, and the Night Market. Each one has its own crowd and its own busy hours.
- Take online orders by courier or ride-hail, and keep your partner rating up.
- Open up to 3 branches, hire staff, and send them stock before you open each day.
- Use the bank for term deposits and loans. Go negative for three closings in a row and the cart is gone.

## The story of Gang Melati

The cart belonged to Mbah Kardi, who sold here for forty years. In the drawer is a recipe book written by his late wife, Sri. Regulars like Pak Gito the motorbike-taxi driver and Bu Surti, who hears every rumor first, stop by to chat. Then a developer arrives with plans to build shophouses right where your cart stands.

Serve people well, learn their favorite dishes, and get closer to them. Near the end you make a choice that decides what happens to the cart. There are 4 choices and 5 endings. The story never blocks your business: you can keep selling after it ends.

## Details

- Languages: English and Bahasa Indonesia (switch any time in Settings → Language).
- Plays in the browser on desktop and mobile. Touch and mouse both work.
- Progress saves automatically in your browser. Optional account sign-in lets you continue on another device and appear on the leaderboard.
- 16 achievements, 19 daily events, daily reports, and a 60-day sales history.

## Controls

- Drag or WASD / arrow keys to move the camera, scroll or pinch to zoom, F to focus on the cart.
- B: Shop · P: Prep · O: Online orders · H: Guide · N: Village News · Space / 1 / 2 / 3: time speed · Esc: menu.
